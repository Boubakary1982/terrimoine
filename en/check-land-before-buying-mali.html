How to Check Land Before Buying in Mali: The Checklist

Many land disputes come from a purchase made without checks. Our checklist guides you point by point before you pay.

Open the free tool

How it works

  1. Identify the real owner and their documents.
  2. Check the title or land document.
  3. Have the plot marked out.
  4. Look for possible disputes or double sales.
  5. Pay through a traceable channel, with a receipt and a notary.

Frequently asked questions

Which documents should you ask the seller for?

The land title or land document, an ID, and the history of the plot. Have everything checked by a notary.

Why have the plot marked out?

To know the exact boundaries and avoid conflicts with neighbours.

What if the seller is in a hurry?

Be wary. Pay nothing without verification and a receipt.
