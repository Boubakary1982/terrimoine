Frequently asked questions

How does the simulator estimate the land's value?

It multiplies the median price per m² of the chosen neighbourhood (Terrimoine Q3 2026 report) by the area you enter, then applies the uncertainty margin you indicate.

Are the three scenarios forecasts?

No. They are annual growth rates you can edit. The starting values (2, 5 and 8%) are calculation examples, not Terrimoine forecasts.

Why an uncertainty margin?

Because a neighbourhood median price does not tell the price of a specific plot. The default margin is ±20%, you can change it.

Does the result replace a valuation?

No. For a firm price, call on a professional valuer or a notary. The simulator gives an order of magnitude.
