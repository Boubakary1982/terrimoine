Frequently asked questions

What is the most expensive land price in Bamako?

In the Terrimoine Q3 2026 survey, Hamdallaye ACI is the most expensive at 525,000 F CFA per m².

Where can I find cheap land in Bamako?

Yirimadio and Sirakoro show the lowest prices in the survey, around 30,000 F CFA per m². The land there is farther from the centre and requires careful title checks.

Where do these prices come from?

From a field network of professionals and listings recorded by Terrimoine. The Q3 2026 edition is a pilot: samples are small and the figures indicative.
