The land title in Mali: the guide to securing your land

Since the land reform (Ordonnance n°2020-014/PT-RM of December 2020, ratified in October 2021), the land title is the only valid proof of ownership. Land without a title remains fragile, even if you have paid and even if you have occupied it for a long time.

Good to know: this guide is general information, written to help you understand. Required documents, deadlines and costs change (the Guichet Unique du Foncier, the one-stop land office, is being digitalised). Always confirm them with the Guichet Unique du Foncier, a notary or a licensed surveyor before acting.

Why the land title is essential

  • It proves you are the owner, not merely an occupant.
  • It protects against double sales and challenges.
  • It lets you sell, pass the property on to your heirs and offer it as security to a bank.

The documents you meet

Depending on the land's history, you may see an allocation letter, an occupancy permit, a private sale deed or receipts. These papers can count in a file, but they do not replace a land title. When a seller shows only such documents, caution is needed.

The main steps to secure a plot

  1. Take stock of the land's situation: who it belongs to, which papers exist, whether there is a dispute.
  2. Gather the documents: IDs, land documents, proof of payment.
  3. Have the plot marked out by a licensed surveyor, to fix the exact boundaries.
  4. File the application with the Guichet Unique du Foncier or the competent administration, with a notary's help if possible.
  5. Follow the file and keep the receipts until the title is issued.

The exact order and documents may vary with your case. Ask for the up-to-date list at the office or from your notary.

The traps to avoid

  • Double sale: the same land sold to two people.
  • The seller with no standing: he is not the real owner.
  • Paying without a receipt or without going through a notary.
  • Imposed urgency: “you must decide today” is a warning sign.
  • The unmarked plan: blurred boundaries create conflicts with neighbours.

Frequently asked questions

Is a land title required to sell land?

Since the land reform, the land title is the only valid proof of ownership. Without it the sale is fragile and a prudent buyer, or his bank, will hesitate. Confirm your situation with a notary.

Is an allocation letter equal to a land title?

No. It can be part of the file, but it does not replace the land title.

How long does it take to get a land title?

It depends on the file and the office. Ask the Guichet Unique du Foncier or a notary for an estimated time.

Who can help me have my land marked out?
