Investing in Mali real estate from abroad

Living far away should not stop you from investing at home, but it takes method: check before paying, have a trusted person on the ground and follow every step.

Good to know: this guide is general information, written to help you understand. Required documents, deadlines and costs change (the Guichet Unique du Foncier, the one-stop land office, is being digitalised). Always confirm them with the Guichet Unique du Foncier, a notary or a licensed surveyor before acting.

1. Define your goal

Future home, rental, building for the family or resale: the goal decides the neighbourhood and the budget. Test scenarios with the yield simulator and the “My budget” tool in the app.

2. Choose the neighbourhood with data

Compare prices and rents on the prices by neighbourhood page. Central neighbourhoods are expensive and scarce, the outskirts are more affordable but need more checks.

3. Have a trusted person on the ground

A professional (notary, surveyor, agency) or a reliable relative must visit, check and send you photos and videos. If you give a mandate or power of attorney, have a notary draft it and limit it to the intended task.

4. Check before any money transfer

  • The seller is really the owner.
  • The land title exists and matches the land.
  • The land is not in dispute.
  • Payment goes through a traceable channel, with a receipt and a notary.
Pre-purchase checklist

5. Build from a distance

Estimate the budget with the materials quote, choose a contractor or site supervisor on a written quote, pay in stages tied to progress and ask for regular video visits.

6. Have the property managed

A rental management agency collects the rents, maintains the property and reports to you. Ask for the terms in writing.

7. Risks to keep in mind

  • Scams linked to fake documents and double sales.
  • The security and economic context: follow the news before committing large sums.
  • Taxation in your country of residence: seek advice from an adviser.

Frequently asked questions

Can I buy land in Mali without going there?

It is possible with a trusted person on the ground and a notary, but you must check the title and the seller before any payment.

How do I avoid scams?

Pay nothing without checking the documents, without a receipt and without a notary. Beware of urgency and prices that are too low.

Who can manage my property in my absence?

A rental management agency. Ask for its terms, fees and reports in writing.
