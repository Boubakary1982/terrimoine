1. Key takeaways

  • A three-speed market. The median price per m² is 200,000 F in the centre and prime residential areas, 150,000 F in established residential areas and 45,000 F on the outskirts.
  • Hamdallaye ACI stands alone at the top. At 525,000 F/m², it is worth 4.2 times the Bamako median. No other tracked neighbourhood comes close.
  • A plateau at 200,000 F/m². 4 neighbourhoods (Bamako Coura, Badalabougou, Quartier du Fleuve, Baco Djicoroni) show the same recorded median price.
  • Rents: from 40,000 to 75,000 F per month for an apartment, from 125,000 to 300,000 F for a villa. A villa rents for a median 3.8 times as much as an apartment in the same neighbourhood.
  • The outskirts offer the widest land availability, but require careful title checks.

6. What this report does not yet say

  • Rental yield by neighbourhood. It requires comparing a rent with the price of a built property. Our current prices are land prices: comparing them with housing rents would give a wrong figure. We will publish it once we have enough sale records of built properties.
  • The price of a specific plot. It depends on the street, access, title and negotiation.
  • Neighbourhoods outside the survey. Unlisted neighbourhoods are not yet covered.

Frequently asked questions

What is the average price per m² in Bamako?

In the Terrimoine Q3 2026 survey, the median price is 123,750 F CFA per m² across 12 neighbourhoods, from 30,000 F in Yirimadio and Sirakoro to 525,000 F in Hamdallaye ACI. We publish the median, which is less sensitive to extreme values than the average.

Are property prices rising in Bamako?

We cannot honestly say yet: Q3 2026 is our first survey. The quarterly change will be published with the Q4 2026 survey, then the trend will sharpen with each report.

Does Terrimoine publish price forecasts?

Not while real history is insufficient. The simulator lets you test your own growth assumptions. These are calculation scenarios, not Terrimoine forecasts.

How reliable are these figures?

Indicative for the Q3 2026 pilot edition: samples are small. Each neighbourhood carries a reliability indicator, and the criteria are published on this page.

Why not show rental yield by neighbourhood?

Because it requires comparing a rent with the price of a built property, whereas our current prices are land prices. We will publish it when we have enough sales of built properties.
