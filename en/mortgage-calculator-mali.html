Frequently asked questions

How is the monthly payment calculated?

With the classic formula for a constant-payment loan, from the amount, the annual rate and the term.

Is the rate my bank's rate?

No. Enter the rate your bank offers you. Rates vary between institutions and with your file.

Is the result a loan offer?

No. It is an indicative simulation. Only the bank can make you an offer.
