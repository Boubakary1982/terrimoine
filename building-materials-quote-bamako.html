Frequently asked questions

How accurate is the quote?

About ±20%. It is a budget-preparation tool. A quote from a contractor or engineer is still necessary.

Where do the prices come from?

Cement, steel, sand and gravel prices follow INSTAT of June 2026. Other prices are assumptions you can edit.
