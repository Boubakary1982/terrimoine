How much does land cost in Bamako Coura?

At the median price, a 200 m² plot comes to about 40,000,000 F CFA and a 300 m² plot to about 60,000,000 F CFA. This simple calculation includes neither transfer fees, titling fees nor professional fees. Compared with the median of the twelve tracked neighbourhoods (123,750 F/m²), the price in Bamako Coura is 1.6 times the median.

Rents in Bamako Coura

For rentals, the pilot survey gives a median rent of about 55,000 F CFA per month for an apartment and 200,000 F CFA per month for a villa. A villa rents for about 3.6 times as much as an apartment in this neighbourhood.

Land availability

Land availability is judged limited in the “Established residential” zone. The scarcer it is, the more prices tend to rise and the more careful you must be about the validity of titles.
